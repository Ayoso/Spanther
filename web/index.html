SPANTHER

    
Meteors are falling on Earth. Look at one and hold your gaze until it bursts.

    

      
SPANTHER is a game that trains attention and concentration in children aged 6 to 10. The child controls it with their eyes.

      
How to play: look at a meteor and keep looking. A ring fills up, and when it closes the meteor bursts. The bigger the meteor, the longer you need to look.

      
This page is a web demo. Without a camera you can play with your finger or mouse. The full version is an iPhone app with eye tracking through ARKit.
